About Me

I am currently an Assistant Professor in the School of Computer Applications at KIIT Deemed to be University, Bhubaneswar, Odisha. I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Hyderabad, under the supervision of Prof. Siba K. Udgata in the School of Computer and Information Sciences. From 2015 to 2016, I served as a Lecturer in the Information Technology and Management (ITM) Department at Ravenshaw University, Cuttack, Odisha. From 2013 to 2015, I was working as Senior Reserach Fellow at Tezpur Central University. in a bioinformatics Project funded by the Department of Biotechnology (DBT), Govt. of India. under the mentorship of Dr. Siddhartha S. Satapathy, Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering.

My research interests include Intelligent Sensing, Internet of Things (IoT), and Device-Free Wi-Fi Sensing.
